WHO WE ARE

TED Capital Holding Group is a comprehensive cross-border holding group with fintech at its core. The Group deeply cultivates global payments, digital banking, cross-border investment, and bulk trade, taking cutting-edge technology as the engine and the highest security standards as the cornerstone to build a two-way empowerment system that connects real industries with global capital markets.

泰德资本控股集团是一家以金融科技为核心的综合性跨国控股集团。集团深耕全球支付、数字银行、跨境投资与大宗贸易领域，以前沿科技为引擎、最高安全标准为基石，构建贯通实体产业与全球资本市场的双向赋能体系。
